# PipOS — Locked Product Flow

## One-sentence concept
A whimsical early-2000s magical computer owned by Pip, a baby skull-faced Junior Dream Engineer who crafts bedtime experiences for the Midnight Nursery.

## Site map
1. WELCOME: Desktop opens. Pip waves, introduces themself and says "I build the dreams you fall asleep inside." Pointer tilt; touch tap reaction; clear hint to scroll.
2. ORIGIN: Three sequential short scenes: (a) a dim star, (b) Pip invents a first light, (c) a constellation and the Midnight Nursery. Scroll-based, concise copy, not a text wall.
3. WORKSHOP: The desktop has three clearly labelled projects; all must be usable: Lullaby Lab (music/play with visible mute), Nightlight Collection (illuminate room with touch/pointer + keyboard alternative), Dream Delivery (select one of three houses; glowing path animation).
4. GOODNIGHT: Pip falls asleep; gently dim the nursery; clickable moon hides a delightful secret dream; visible credits.

## Navigation
Main page has genuine vertical scroll. The desktop treatment is visual language, NOT a full operating-system simulation. Desktop project launcher opens one accessible project panel at a time. On mobile, windows are stackable full-width panels; no drag requirement, no hover-only functionality. Every project can be reached from keyboard and touch.

## Two signature moments
1. A high-quality light interaction revealing hidden, cozy objects in a dark room (strongest interaction).
2. The transition from star to constellation during Pip's short origin story (strongest storytelling).

## Scope
MVP includes all six competition requirements plus the three distinct project interactions. Extra cursor sparkle and sound click are polish. Omit backend, chatbots, realistic 3D, complex draggable window manager and unnecessary dependencies.

## Pass/fail
A new visitor can understand who Pip is and what Pip does within the first screen, discover their story by scrolling, use three projects, trigger an Easter egg, mute sounds, and complete the experience on a phone without horizontal scrolling or broken controls.
